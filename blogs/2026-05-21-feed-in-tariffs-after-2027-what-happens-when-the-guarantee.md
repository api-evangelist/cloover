Cloover’s Head of Operations and Energy, Florent Erdbar, joined the inaugural episode of the My Solar Express (MSE) podcast at the invitation of host Dimitri Kuzin. The question they tackled is one the whole industry is sitting with: How does photovoltaic hold up over the next 20 years once today’s support structure is gone? At a glance: PV systems commissioned by 31 December 2026 would lock in Germany’s fixed feed-in tariff for 20 years – under the reform as currently drafted.
